# Owner judgments — employer payroll allocations and call priorities

Recorded November 5, 2026, from the project owner’s stated judgments and subsequent amount clarification. The first-person statements below represent the owner’s reasoning, edited for clarity. Supporting links were added for traceability; they do not imply that the owner independently checked every linked record.

## Scope of my review

I reviewed the [evidence brief](docs/evidence-brief.md) and the employer application files. I am judging the allocations supplied by the employers; I have **not independently recalculated every record**. My decisions remain provisional wherever documentation or policy confirmation is outstanding.

Recording these judgments does **not** close business issues, confirm missing evidence, or establish audit sign-off.

**My limitation:** The receipts period does not exactly match the audit period. Exact-period evidence or approval of the provisional proxy remains necessary.

Supporting references: [application][ll-app]; [receipts][ll-receipts]; [evidence brief, Larkspur](docs/evidence-brief.md#2-larkspur--catering-threshold-and-receipts-period); [interview 2](interviews/interview-2.md), 12:34–12:35 AM. ## My two-company call ranking

### First — Severn Homebuilders

Specific missing documents could change a material proposed payroll addition. Further analysis of the same records cannot establish missing coverage or legal identity. That makes obtaining evidence more valuable than continuing to analyze what is already supplied.

Evidence underlying this priority: the [payments][sv-payments] compared with the [supplied certificates][sv-certs], as presented in the evidence brief. Missing proof is not proof that the subcontractors were uninsured.

### Second — Larkspur Lane Bistro

Exact-period receipts could resolve a material classification uncertainty. I am prioritizing the **evidence gap**, not predicting that the percentage will cross 40%. The employer would supply the receipts; Nadia would address any proxy or policy approval.

Evidence underlying this priority: the [receipts summary][ll-receipts] and its period mismatch, together with Nadia’s threshold and period-alignment statements in [interview 2](interviews/interview-2.md), 12:34–12:35 AM.

### Why these two, and when I would reconsider

The other four employers’ main corrections or employee treatment are more readily supported by the existing records. Follow-up remains useful because they still have unresolved issues, but I judge Severn’s and Larkspur’s evidence gaps to be more severe priorities for the two recommended calls.

I would change the ranking if missing documents arrive or new evidence reveals a more consequential unresolved issue elsewhere.
