Use with the freshly retrieved company guide and original auditor interview. This reference describes the supported rules, not replacement source evidence. The project owner approved P1–P7 as provisional treatments; that approval does not settle individual open cases.

Specific scenarios above take precedence over generic CG-07 splitting. Do not apply the roofing rule to unrelated trades. Ordinary direct workers use the guide code matching their actual work. Code-description identifiers must be extracted from the current guide, not synthesized from general-rule paragraph numbers.

## Preserve open treatments

- **P1:** legal-support visits with no established field trade may provisionally retain C-110, with actual visits and the office-wording exception disclosed. Never erase visits or invent a construction code.
- **P2:** unique positive-time dates, corrected field totals before office transfers, and unresolved tie/no-field treatment are explicit calculation choices. A no-field exception must be proposed and justified, not automatic.
- **P3:** unapproved contractor aliases do not prove coverage. Include 60% provisionally when full-period coverage is absent; keep identity/coverage open until sufficient confirmation/evidence arrives.
- **P4:** apply CG-05 first for a valid exclusion, then the working-owner rule for included field owners. Missing election documentation leaves an off-register omission provisional with amount null and final sign-off blocked. Do not invent duties, draws, or $63,700 for a non-field owner.
- **P5:** allowance with zero days and no documented reimbursement is provisionally included under the worker's trade; preserve an open question about expense and travel evidence.
- **P6:** nonmatching receipts periods may be used only as an explicit approved provisional proxy. Seek boundary transactions or audit-period totals; never fabricate daily prorations.
- **P7:** one suitable confirmation from Nadia or the employer can resolve the relevant item. Record who, when, what, and evidence. Confirmation of one record does not resolve unrelated issues or imply all exclusions are documented.

All source-backed adjustments and relied-on records still need named confirmation questions. Unknown amounts remain null in decision records and “unknown” in prose. Keep provisional CSVs accompanied by the report; completion of calculations never grants audit sign-off.
